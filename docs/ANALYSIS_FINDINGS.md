# Analysis Findings

> These findings describe the **modeled portfolio dataset**, not confidential Manchester City performance.

## Executive readout
- Modeled fan base: **30,000**; members: **10,141** (33.8%).
- Ticket buyers: **14,184**; modeled repeat-attendance rate: **55.6%**.
- Modeled known revenue: **£5,334,270**, split between ticket **£2,906,637**, retail **£1,418,266**, and membership **£1,009,367**.
- Modeled CRM CTR: **6.8%**; delivered-to-conversion: **0.8%**.

## Commercial interpretation
The model is designed to expose relationships between engagement, membership and purchase—not to manufacture a single “club revenue” number. The useful management question is which cohorts have intent signals but under-convert commercially, then whether targeted interventions lift conversion.

## Opportunity lenses
1. **International digital-only fans** — prioritise content, membership and retail propositions before assuming matchday conversion is realistic.
2. **UK developing fans** — test membership-to-ticket conversion and repeat-attendance nudges.
3. **Retail-only customers** — use product affinity to broaden the club relationship.
4. **Dormant fans** — use controlled win-back rather than expensive blanket outreach.
5. **Match categories** — analyse yield and repeat behaviour, not occupancy alone.
