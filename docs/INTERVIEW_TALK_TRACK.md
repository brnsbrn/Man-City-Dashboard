# Interview Talk Track

“I built a football fan-intelligence model around acquisition, engagement, membership, ticketing, retail and CRM conversion. Customer-level Manchester City data is private, so I separated public benchmarks from synthetic microdata rather than pretending dummy rows were real. I modeled the star schema, KPI logic in SQL/DAX, and six decision pages in Power BI. The key use case is tracing engagement through commercial behaviour and identifying lifecycle/segment opportunities.”

Be ready to explain: why synthetic data; how public benchmarks constrain assumptions; why audited club revenue is not added to sample transaction revenue; what would change with real CRM data; and what experiment/action each insight should trigger.
